# JiEnzi Africa Design References

These notes preserve direction for upcoming design-system and homepage tasks. They are references, not templates to copy.

## Primary Reference: Buzz Women

Source: https://www.buzzwomen.org/

Use Buzz Women as the strongest reference for:

- Movement-led organizational storytelling.
- Expressive identity rooted in real people and community action.
- Authentic human photography that feels present, warm, and specific.
- A homepage rhythm that varies section formats instead of repeating cards.
- Invitations to participate that feel like joining a shared cause.
- Clear organization-wide framing, not a single-program identity.

Avoid copying its branding, content, language, layouts, or exact interaction patterns.

## Secondary Reference: Guided Growth Learning

Source: https://www.guidedgrowthlearningco.com/

Use Guided Growth Learning as a lighter reference for:

- Explaining a growth approach in clear steps.
- Making a methodology feel practical and approachable.
- Presenting experiences and participation paths without overcomplication.
- Guiding visitors from understanding to action.

Avoid copying its visual style, wording, testimonials, or program framing.

## JiEnzi Africa Direction

The JiEnzi Africa site should feel welcoming, energetic, culturally grounded, professional, and alive. It should represent real young Africans and the broader JiEnzi Africa organization, not only JiEnzi Skates.

Design priorities:

- Bold but natural typography.
- Authentic, people-centered imagery.
- Purposeful layout variation.
- Brand-consistent use of yellow, near-black, off-white, and white.
- Responsive mobile and desktop experiences.
- Clear organizational storytelling around Reflection -> Proactivity -> Intentionality.
- Tasteful motion and interaction only where it supports meaning.
- Accessibility and performance.

Constraints:

- Do not use generic corporate or nonprofit templates.
- Do not rely on repetitive homepage cards.
- Do not add excessive gradients, shadows, or decorative effects.
- Do not invent statistics, testimonials, partner claims, or program outcomes.
- Do not present stock photography as actual JiEnzi activity.
- Do not reduce JiEnzi Africa to its skating initiative.
- Do not copy either reference site's branding or content.

Task 001 remains limited to the project foundation. These notes are for future design-system and homepage tasks.
